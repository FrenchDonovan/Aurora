## Accepted research/CV-ready blockers

- The eight brief abstract excerpts cannot substantiate a completed flagship research assistant. Expand a narrower licensed context-preserving domain, proposed as quantization trade-offs, before claiming meaningful question-answering capability.
- Human-reviewed relevance/answerability evidence is required; AI-generated relation labels are not human gold. No production model is supplied.
- Model benefit needs genuine controls and an empirical comparison. Training a tiny ranker or exporting weights alone is insufficient.
- A source-removal study needs unique, independently redundant, irrelevant/contextual and sham-removal cases with reviewed expected behavior. Mechanical deletion is tautological evidence of text removal.
- Complete metadata ablation must remove title/tag contributions at candidate scoring, features and gate, rather than drop only one final feature.
- Statistical reporting must reflect query families, incomplete judgments, contextual/direct relevance, unanswerable populations and limited power. Tiny convenience samples remain pilot evidence.
- Unknown third-party reuse permission is recorded per source. Larger context must be licensed/reviewed; a short excerpt policy is not a blanket full-text permission.
- A private preview is not a public CV link. Unauthenticated public access, rights/content review and research gates remain separate release requirements.
